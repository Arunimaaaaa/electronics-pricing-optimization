## 🚀 Quick Start (Right Now!)

1. **Open Dashboard**
   - Go to: http://localhost:8501

2. **Select a Product**
   - Use left sidebar dropdown
   - Pick any product (Smartphone, Laptop, etc.)

3. **Choose Your Goal**
   - Click "Revenue" or "Profit"

4. **Go to Optimization Tab**
   - Click the "Optimization" tab (4th tab)
   - Look for the RED DASHED LINE
   - That's your answer!

5. **Apply the Price**
   - Update your store to the recommended price

**Time Needed:** 5 minutes ⏱️
