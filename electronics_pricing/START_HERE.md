- Disadvantage: Fewer customers

## What Each AI Model Does (Simple Version)

**Linear Regression:** Draws a straight line through data
- Easiest to understand
- Good for simple patterns

**Random Forest:** Asks 100 "experts" and votes
- More accurate
- Harder to explain

**XGBoost:** Learns from mistakes progressively
- Most powerful
- Very accurate

**Which One Does System Use?** The most accurate one for each product!

## The Numbers That Matter

### R² Score (Confidence Level)
```
0.8 - 1.0 = Excellent (Trust it!)      ✅✅✅
0.6 - 0.8 = Good (Fair guidance)       ✅✅
0.4 - 0.6 = Fair (Use caution)         ✅
0.0 - 0.4 = Low (Use gut feeling)
```
